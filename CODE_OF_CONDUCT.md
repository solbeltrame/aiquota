# Code of Conduct

AIquota adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want participation in AIquota to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation. First-time contributors are welcome here.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the people using AIquota.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainers through GitHub's
[private reporting](../../security/advisories/new) or by contacting a maintainer directly. All reports will be
reviewed promptly and confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or other contributions, or
temporarily or permanently ban contributors who violate this Code of Conduct.
